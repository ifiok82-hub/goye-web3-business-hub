Public Notice: This privacy policy is public, freely accessible, mobile-friendly, and does not require an account, login, or Pi authentication to view. GOYE maintains strict non-custodial architecture: we never ask for, hold, or store Pi Wallet passphrases, private keys, or seed phrases.

1. Introduction & Operational Scope

GOYE Web3 Business Hub ("GOYE", "we", "our", or "the Platform") operates business infrastructure providing cryptographic corporate identity verification, digital commercial invoicing, milestone contract record-keeping, and enterprise AI orchestration for global and African digital commerce.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, processed, recorded, and safeguarded when visiting https://goye-web3-business-hub.vercel.app, interacting through standard web browsers or the official Pi Browser, or engaging with our business verification and payment interfaces.

2. Corporate & Business Information Collected

When registering for a GOYE Business ID or publishing a commercial profile, businesses submit:

  • Commercial Identity: Entity name, brand trading name, corporate registration number, jurisdiction (Country and State/Province), and official business address.
  • Communication Channels: Business contact email, corporate telephone or WhatsApp dispatch number, and official website URL.
  • Catalog & Offerings: Product and service listings, descriptive deliverables, pricing schedules, and fulfillment timelines.
  • Verification Proofs: Optional corporate registration documentation or certificates uploaded to verify compliance levels.

Business profile information published to the Enterprise Marketplace or Business ID portal is made visible to prospective clients for commercial transparency.

3. Pi Network Authentication Information

GOYE Web3 Business Hub integrates with the official Pi Network Platform SDK and APIs for Pioneer identity verification:

  • Scopes Requested: We request only username and payments scopes through Pi.authenticate().
  • Data Processed: Pi User Identifier (UID) and public Pi username (e.g., @username).
  • Security Rule: GOYE systems NEVER request, intercept, handle, or store your Pi Wallet passphrase, secret seed, or private key. Authentication is performed exclusively inside the secure Pi Browser sandbox.

4. Payment Processing & Testnet Transactions

Our payment architecture adheres strictly to official Pi Platform API specifications:

  • Network Mode: Development operations use Pi Testnet (0.1 Test-Pi validation). Test-Pi possesses no monetary or real-world value.
  • Two-Phase Approval: Payments are approved by our backend server (POST /api/pi/approve) and finalized upon blockchain confirmation (POST /api/pi/complete).
  • Recorded Metadata: Internal order references, payment identifiers (paymentId), blockchain transaction hashes (txid), timestamps, and settlement statuses.
  • Zero-Simulation Guarantee: GOYE does not simulate payments, forge transaction hashes, or create counterfeit completion statuses.

5. Document Uploads & Storage Security

Businesses utilizing the GOYE Document Vault or Trade Assurance milestones upload commercial proofs (invoices, receipts, bilateral agreements):

  • Files are cryptographically hashed (SHA-256) upon upload to generate immutable integrity checksums.
  • Uploaded documents are restricted to authenticated business owners and designated audit compliance officers.
  • Files are never sold, rented, or distributed to third-party data brokers.

6. Artificial Intelligence & Automated Features

GOYE provides autonomous AI assistants (Receptionist, Sales Assistant, Support, FAQ) powered by enterprise language models:

  • AI assistants answer inquiries strictly using verified business records and published public catalogs.
  • Inbound prospect inquiries submitted to AI widgets (contact info, inquiry intent, budget) are routed exclusively to the respective business owner.
  • User prompt data is never used to train public foundational AI models without authorization.

7. Cookies, Local Storage & Session Identifiers

GOYE Web3 Business Hub uses minimal, functional storage mechanisms:

  • Local Storage: Storing active theme preferences, PWA install dismissals, and client-side view states.
  • Session Tokens: Temporary cryptographic session tokens validating active Pioneer authentication sessions.
  • No Third-Party Ad Trackers: We do not employ third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or invasive telemetry beacons.

8. Security Architecture & Audit Immutability

To prevent commercial fraud and maintain dispute readiness:

  • All payment approvals, contract signatures, verification updates, and profile modifications generate immutable audit log records.
  • Sensitive environment variables (such as PI_API_KEY and server credentials) are isolated strictly to backend execution environments and never exposed to client browsers.
  • All web communications are enforced over secure Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / TLS 1.3).

9. Data Retention & Archival Policies

Commercial transaction receipts, invoices, and verified contract timestamps are retained to fulfill operational integrity and regulatory accounting obligations. Verified businesses may request profile archiving, data export, or record rectification by contacting compliance administration.

10. Pioneer & Merchant Rights

Every user, merchant, and Pioneer maintains the right to:

  • Access, inspect, and export their registered business profile and commercial ledger.
  • Update, correct, or amend operational hours, contact details, or catalog items.
  • Disconnect their Pioneer session or sign out at any time.
  • Report inaccurate business claims or dispute fraudulent listings through our Trade Assurance mediation channels.

11. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

The Platform interacts with trusted external infrastructure:

  • Pi Network (SocialChain Inc.): For Pioneer OAuth and official Platform API payment validation (https://api.minepi.com).
  • Vercel Inc.: Hosting production edge deployment and serverless API execution under strict transport security.
